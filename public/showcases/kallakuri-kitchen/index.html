OUR HERITAGE

Bringing Traditional Recipes to Modern Presentation.

Chef Meenakshi Sundaram spent decades mastering the ancestral spice blends of Chettinad and the delicate ferments of Malabar. At Kallakuri Kitchen, she translates these deep-rooted traditions into a modern visual language, ensuring every plate is both a memory and a discovery.
