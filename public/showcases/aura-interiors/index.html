What We Do

Our Expertise

From heritage homes to contemporary workspaces, we craft environments that honor tradition while embracing modern living. Every project begins with listening — understanding your story, your rituals, and the way you inhabit space.

Our approach blends Kerala's architectural legacy with global design sensibilities, creating interiors that feel both timeless and distinctly yours.

The Designer

Curated by Meera Nair

Meera Nair founded Aura Interiors with a singular vision: to create spaces that breathe with the soul of their inhabitants. Trained at NID Ahmedabad and with over a decade of practice across India and Southeast Asia, Meera brings a rare sensitivity to material, light, and memory.

Her philosophy is rooted in the belief that great design is invisible — it doesn't announce itself, but rather makes you feel exactly where you are meant to be. Every Aura project carries this quiet confidence, this sense of belonging.
