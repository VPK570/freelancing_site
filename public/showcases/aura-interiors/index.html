Founder's Philosophy

Meera Nair

With a career spanning two decades across the architectural landscapes of Europe and Asia, Meera Nair founded Aura Interiors to bridge the gap between traditional heritage and modern luxury.

Her approach, often described as 'Royal-Rustic', favors the use of authentic, raw materials—reclaimed wood, honed marble, and artisanal metals—sculpted into sophisticated, contemporary forms.

Meera believes that true luxury is not about excess, but about the intentional curation of one's environment to reflect a storied life.
